What this website is

greetro.com is a marketing and waitlist website for Greetro, an AI voice receptionist we are building. Using this site means reading these pages and, if you choose, adding yourself to the waitlist. That is the whole of it.

Greetro is not launched yet

This matters more than anything else on this page, so we will be blunt about it. As of 2026-09-19:

  • There is no Greetro service answering calls.
  • There are no accounts, no logins, and no dashboard.
  • There is no checkout, no subscription, and no free trial. Nothing on this site can charge you.
  • Joining the waitlist is not a purchase, an order, or a contract. It creates no obligation on either side.

They are forward-looking statements about software in development, not a warranty of current capability, and specifics may change as we build.
