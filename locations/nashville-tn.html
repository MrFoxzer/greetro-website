The local picture

Why Nashville businesses stop missing calls

Nashville's boom runs from The Gulch to East Nashville and out to Franklin — new residents pick clinics, salons, and firms by who picks up. Wherever your callers come from — The Gulch, East Nashville, Green Hills, and Franklin — they expect a fast pickup and a real answer, not a voicemail greeting.

Greetro is a cloud service available to any business with a Nashville phone line. There’s no office visit and nothing to install: forward your existing number and it starts answering with your greeting, your rules, and your calendar.

A full front desk for your Nashville line

Emergencies routed to your on-call line

No more “who called?” Your dashboard logs every Nashville call with who, what, and what happened — transcript included, summary on top.
