FAQ

Questions, answered

How natural does Greetro actually sound?

Greetro uses modern neural voices with natural pacing, pauses, and interruption handling — callers can talk over it, change their mind mid-sentence, or ask something unexpected, and it keeps up. It always identifies itself honestly as an automated assistant if a caller asks.

What happens when a caller really needs a human?

You define what counts as urgent — a medical emergency, a burst pipe, a caller who simply insists. Greetro transfers those calls to your on-call number immediately and whispers a one-line summary to whoever picks up. Everything else becomes a booking or a structured message.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Keep your existing number and forward calls to Greetro — always, only when busy, or only after hours. You can also add a fresh local number if you prefer. Turning forwarding off returns everything to exactly how it was.

Which calendars and tools does it work with?

Greetro syncs two-way with Google Calendar and Outlook on the Pro and Scale plans, so it only ever offers genuinely open slots. The Scale plan adds CRM integrations and an API for pushing call outcomes into the systems you already run.

What languages does Greetro speak?

30+ languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Hindi, and Mandarin. It detects the caller’s language automatically and answers in kind — one receptionist for every caller you serve.
